# Staking on DEXRise

DEXRise staking lets you put your tokens to work and earn rewards — with full flexibility over which assets you stake. Pools can be built from **any two currencies**, whether they are **different currencies or of the same type**, giving projects and users a wide range of pool designs.

All staking activity runs through a dedicated [Staking Panel](#the-staking-panel) and settles **directly on-chain**, with full transparency and security.

## Key characteristics

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Pool composition** | Any two currencies — different currencies or the same currency on both sides |
| **Contract types** | Two staking models: [Flexible](staking-types.md#flexible-staking) and [Locked](staking-types.md#locked-staking) |
| **Reward model** | [APR-based](apr-mechanics.md) — calculated over a full 365-day year, with simple (non-compounded) interest |
| **Execution** | Stake, withdraw and claim operations settle directly on-chain |

## The Staking Panel

The Staking Panel is designed to be simple and easy to use — the three core actions of staking are each a **one-click operation**:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Deposit** | Stake your tokens into the pool |
| **Withdraw** | Pull your deposit out of the pool — availability depends on the contract type |
| **Claim** | Collect your accumulated rewards at any time |

## Choosing a contract type

Every staking pool runs on one of two contract types, and the difference comes down to how the deposit is treated:

* **[Flexible](staking-types.md#flexible-staking)** — a flexible-term contract: withdraw your deposit plus rewards **at any time**, with no restrictions.
* **[Locked](staking-types.md#locked-staking)** — a fixed-term contract: the deposit stays in the pool until the staking period fully ends, while **rewards remain claimable at any time** or can be redeposited.

{% hint style="info" %}
Rewards are calculated as [simple interest at a stated APR](apr-mechanics.md) — the rate is annualized over 365 full days, and rewards are computed on your deposited amount only, without compounding.
{% endhint %}
