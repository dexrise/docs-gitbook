# Pair Ranks

DEXRise classifies trading pairs and currencies into **four rank tiers**: **Bronze**, **Silver**, **Gold** and **Platinum**. Ranks give you an at-a-glance signal of a pair's community support and trading activity, helping you evaluate pairs before trading them.

## The four tiers

| Rank | Votes | Daily trading volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🥉 **Bronze** | More than 100 votes | — |
| 🥈 **Silver** | More than 1,000 votes | — |
| 🥇 **Gold** | More than 10,000 votes | More than $1,000,000 |
| 💎 **Platinum** | More than 10,000 votes | More than $10,000,000 |

## How to read the tiers

Ranks combine two signals:

* **Votes** — community support behind a pair. A pair climbs from Bronze to Silver to Gold as its vote count grows past each threshold.
* **Daily trading volume** — real trading activity. Volume requirements kick in at the top tiers: Gold requires a daily volume above **$1 million**, and Platinum requires above **$10 million**.

{% hint style="info" %}
Gold and Platinum share the same votes requirement (more than 10,000 votes) — **daily trading volume is what separates them**. A Gold pair with more than $10 million in daily volume qualifies for Platinum.
{% endhint %}

## What each tier communicates

| Tier | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Bronze** | The pair has crossed its first community milestone — an emerging pair with early support |
| **Silver** | A substantially larger support base, in the thousands of votes |
| **Gold** | Strong community support combined with serious trading activity |
| **Platinum** | The platform's top tier — high community support and high daily volume at scale |

## Where ranks appear

Ranks are assigned to trading pairs and currencies across the platform, so you can weigh community strength and activity as part of your trading decisions — alongside the pair details shown in the [Trade Panel](trade-panel.md).
