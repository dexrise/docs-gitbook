# Trade Panel

The Trade Panel is where every swap happens on DEXRise. It is deliberately simple and easy to use, so that both first-time users and experienced traders can move through a trade without friction — while still surfacing every number that matters before the trade is executed.

## What the panel gives you

| Feature | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| **Balance overview** | See your balances directly in the panel, so you always know what you are working with |
| **Live pair price** | The instant price of the selected trading pair, at the moment you are trading |
| **Pre-execution cost calculation** | Calculate the total cost of the trade in advance — before executing the transaction, not after |
| **Fee transparency** | The fee value of the transaction is included in the pre-execution calculation, so there are no surprises after the swap |
| **Simple, focused design** | A clean panel layout that keeps the trading flow fast and easy to understand |

## Why the pre-execution calculation matters

On many platforms, the true cost of a trade only becomes clear after it has already been executed. The DEXRise Trade Panel works the other way around: before you confirm anything, it shows you the calculated cost of the deal **plus the fee value** of the transaction.

This means that by the time you execute a trade, you already know:

1. How much of the currency you are paying with will leave your balance.
2. How much of that amount is [trading fees](trading-fees.md).
3. The effective result of the swap at the current pair price.

{% hint style="info" %}
The exact figures depend on the pair you are trading: fee rates differ between [DEXRise pairs and external pairs](trading-fees.md), and the live price always reflects the liquidity pool behind the pair.
{% endhint %}
