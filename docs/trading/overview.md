# Trading on DEXRise

Trading is the core of the DEXRise platform. Through a single interface, you can swap tokens on liquidity pools belonging to major decentralized exchanges — **PancakeSwap**, **Uniswap**, **SushiSwap** and others — as well as on [DEXRise's own native liquidity pools](../introduction/how-it-works.md#native-and-shared-liquidity).

Every pair is presented with rich, easy-to-read detail: live pair pricing, your balances, a cost preview before you execute, and protective settings that keep you in control of each trade.

## Two types of trading pairs

DEXRise offers two categories of trading pairs:

| Pair type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Traditional pairs** | Standard swap pairs available on any decentralized exchange, supported by full liquidity on both sides |
| **Floor Price Protection pairs** | A category exclusive to DEXRise: pairs whose price is protected by an unbreakable, automatically rising price floor |

{% hint style="info" %}
Floor Price Protection is DEXRise's signature feature — it cannot be found on any other platform. Read the dedicated [Floor Price Protection](floor-price-protection.md) page to understand how the floor is defined, how it rises with every new all-time high, and what happens when the price reaches it.
{% endhint %}

## Tools that support every trade

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Trade Panel](trade-panel.md) | View your balances, the live pair price and the exact cost of a trade — fees included — before executing it |
| [Slippage Protection](slippage-protection.md) | Set the maximum price change you are willing to accept; the trade fails automatically beyond that limit |
| [Trading Fees](trading-fees.md) | A transparent fee schedule based on the pair type: 0.3% on DEXRise pairs, 0.4% on external pairs |
| [Pair Ranks](ranks.md) | Community-driven tiering of pairs and currencies: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum |
