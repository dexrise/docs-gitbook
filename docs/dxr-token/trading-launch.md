# DXR Trading Launch

The **DXR/USDT** trading pair is where DXR's market journey begins — and it launches with Floor Price Protection already in force from the very first second.

## Launch schedule

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Pair** | DXR/USDT |
| **Launch date** | **February 1, 2027, at the latest** — **or earlier** if the [presale quantity sells out](presale-event.md) before the end date |
| **Early launch** | If the presale sells out ahead of schedule (for example in January), the trading launch is brought forward accordingly — updates are announced on the [official channels](../contact.md) |

{% hint style="warning" %}
The launch date can move **earlier** — but never later — than February 1, 2027. Follow the [official DEXRise social accounts](../contact.md) to catch any early-launch announcement.
{% endhint %}

## Launch parameters

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Launch price** | **0.001 USDT per DXR** |
| **Floor price at launch** | **0.001 USDT** — equal to the launch price itself |
| **Initial liquidity** | **500,000,000 DXR + 500,000 USDT** |
| **Initial market cap** | **$1,000,000** |
| **Floor Price Protection** | 50% max drawdown from the last ATH |

## Launching at the floor

The launch price and the floor price are the **same level**: DXR starts trading **at its floor directly — 0.001 USDT**.

This is a deliberate design:

* The floor at launch is **unbreakable** — selling cannot push the price below 0.001 USDT from the very first trade.
* The moment trading begins, the price is at the bottom of its protected range — so the **first upward cycle starts immediately** at launch.
* From there, the normal [Floor Price Protection](../trading/floor-price-protection.md) rules take over: as new ATHs are recorded, the floor moves permanently higher, and the previous floor level can never be revisited.

## The launch liquidity

The initial liquidity of **500,000,000 DXR + 500,000 USDT** corresponds to the 50% trading-liquidity allocation in the [tokenomics](tokenomics.md#distribution) — the largest allocation of the supply. It provides immediate trading depth for the market from day one, held at the official [trading liquidity pool address](official-addresses.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
At the launch price of 0.001 USDT, the 1 billion max supply corresponds to an initial market capitalization of **$1,000,000**.
{% endhint %}
