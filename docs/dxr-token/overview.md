# DXR Token Overview

**DXR** is the official token of the DEXRise platform. It is the token at the center of the platform's economy: it anchors the platform's [trading liquidity](tokenomics.md#distribution), powers the platform's [staking program](staking-event.md), and it is protected by DEXRise's signature [Floor Price Protection](../trading/floor-price-protection.md) mechanism at its strongest setting.

## Floor Price Protection at 50%

DXR trades with [Floor Price Protection](../trading/floor-price-protection.md) at **50%**: the floor price always sits 50% below the last recorded ATH, selling stops at the floor, and every new ATH moves the floor permanently higher. The result is a token whose market structure is designed for a continuous upward trend — with the price free to move anywhere within the range between the floor and the ATH.

{% hint style="info" %}
DXR launches trading **at its floor price directly** — the floor is unbreakable from the very first second of trading, so the first upward cycle begins immediately at launch. Full details in [DXR Trading Launch](trading-launch.md).
{% endhint %}
