## What each address holds

Each address maps directly to a [tokenomics allocation](tokenomics.md#distribution):

| Address role | Allocation it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Trading liquidity pool** | 50% of supply — 500,000,000 DXR backing DXR trading pairs |
| **Staking** | 30% of supply — 300,000,000 DXR funding the staking program over 10 years |
| **Presale** | 10% of supply — 100,000,000 DXR allocated to the presale event |
| **Airdrop** | 5% of supply — 50,000,000 DXR for community airdrop events |
| **Team wallet** | 5% of supply — 50,000,000 DXR allocated to the team |

## Safety checklist

* ✅ Verify every address against **this page** before any interaction.
* ✅ Follow announcements only through the [official DEXRise channels](../contact.md).
* ❌ Never trust addresses sent to you in private messages, even if they appear official.
* ❌ DEXRise will never ask for your private keys or seed phrase.
