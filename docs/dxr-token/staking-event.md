# DXR Staking Event

The DXR staking program is one of the largest allocations in the [DXR tokenomics](tokenomics.md#distribution): **300,000,000 DXR — 30% of the total supply — dedicated entirely to staking rewards**, released across a **10-year schedule**. The program opens **during the presale itself**, with its first round running at **100% APR**.

## Program parameters

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Total staking allocation** | 300,000,000 DXR — 30% of max supply |
| **Release schedule** | Distributed across **10 years** |
| **First year release** | 100,000,000 DXR |
| **Subsequent rounds** | Gradually decreasing in the rounds that follow |
| **First round (Round 1)** | November 3, 2026 → November 3, 2027 — a full year |
| **Round 1 APR** | **100%** |

## Round 1 — 100% APR

The first staking round runs for a **full year**, from **November 3, 2026 to November 3, 2027**, at an APR of **100%** — the strongest rate of the program:

* The first-year release of **100 million DXR** makes Round 1 the largest single-year reward pool of the entire 10-year schedule.
* After Round 1, subsequent rounds **decrease gradually**.
* Rewards follow the standard [APR mechanics](../staking/apr-mechanics.md): annualized over 365 full days, calculated as simple interest on the deposited amount.

## Staking during the presale

The staking program is **fully functional while the [DXR Presale Event](presale-event.md) is running**:

* You can participate in staking **during the presale period** — with full functionality from day one.
* The contract type is **[Flexible](../staking/staking-types.md#flexible-staking)** — you can **withdraw your deposit at any time**, with no restrictions.
* **Claim your rewards at any time** — or re-deposit them to keep growing your position while the presale runs.

This combination is what makes the presale especially attractive: participants can buy DXR at the fixed presale price and immediately put it to work at 100% APR. See [DXR Presale Event](presale-event.md#why-participate-in-the-presale) for the full participation picture.
