# DXR Presale Event

The DXR Presale Event is the token sale that opens up DXR participation **before trading launches**. It runs on an **immediate delivery** contract — every purchase delivers DXR instantly — and it connects directly to the [staking program](staking-event.md), which runs in parallel at 100% APR.

## Event parameters

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Sale period** | November 20, 2026 → February 1, 2027 *(maximum end date)* |
| **End condition** | The presale ends when the **maximum period expires** or the **quantity sells out** — whichever comes first |
| **Allocation for sale** | 100,000,000 DXR — 10% of the max [supply](tokenomics.md) |
| **Sale price** | **0.001 USDT per DXR** — fixed |
| **Funding target** | 100,000 USDT |
| **Contract type** | [Immediate delivery](../presale/contract-types.md#immediate-delivery-contracts) — tokens received at the moment of purchase |

## Why participate in the presale

### 1. A fixed entry price

Presale participants buy DXR at a **fixed price of 0.001 USDT per DXR** — the same price at which the token later launches on the market. Your entry price is locked in regardless of when you buy during the event.

### 2. Staking at 100% APR during the presale

The [DXR staking program](staking-event.md) runs **in parallel with the presale**, and participants can join it during the event:

* Stake your DXR at **100% APR** while the presale is still running.
* The staking contract is **[Flexible](../staking/staking-types.md#flexible-staking)** — withdraw your deposit at any time, with no restrictions.
* Claim your [rewards](../staking/apr-mechanics.md) at any time.
* You can keep **increasing your position** by staking throughout the entire presale period — buying during the event and compounding your holding through staking rewards.

{% hint style="info" %}
In effect, a presale participant can hold DXR that is earning 100% APR **from the moment of purchase** — weeks before trading even begins. When trading launches, the floor price sits at the launch price itself (see [DXR Trading Launch](trading-launch.md)).
{% endhint %}

## After the presale

* If tokens remain unsold at the end of the event period, they are **delivered to the team**, following the standard [presale settlement rules](../presale/lifecycle.md#stage-4-settlement).
* Trading on the **DXR/USDT** pair begins **at the latest by February 1, 2027 — or earlier** if the presale sells out ahead of schedule. Details in [DXR Trading Launch](trading-launch.md).

## Stay updated

The launch schedule can move earlier if the presale sells out — announcements are made through the [official DEXRise channels](../contact.md).
