# Tokenomics

DXR has a **fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 DXR**. This maximum supply is **not inflatable** — it cannot be increased, so the token's supply is capped from day one.

## Distribution

The max supply is allocated across five purposes:

| Allocation | Share | Amount | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Trading liquidity** | 50% | 500,000,000 DXR | Backs DXR trading pairs on the platform, starting with the [DXR/USDT launch](trading-launch.md) |
| **Staking** | 30% | 300,000,000 DXR | Rewards for the [DXR staking program](staking-event.md), released across 10 years |
| **Presale** | 10% | 100,000,000 DXR | Sold in the [DXR Presale Event](presale-event.md) at a fixed price |
| **Airdrops** | 5% | 50,000,000 DXR | Funds [airdrop events](../airdrop/overview.md) for the community |
| **Team** | 5% | 50,000,000 DXR | Allocated to the DEXRise team |

## Reading the allocation

* **Half of the entire supply goes to trading liquidity** — the largest single allocation. This anchors the token's trading depth on the platform: the [DXR/USDT launch pool](trading-launch.md) alone starts with 500 million DXR.
* **Nearly a third funds staking rewards** — the 300 million DXR staking allocation is the engine of the [staking program](staking-event.md), deliberately released over a full 10-year schedule so that staking remains rewarding long after launch. The first year releases 100 million DXR, beginning with a 100% APR round.
* **The presale allocation is capped at 10%** — exactly 100 million DXR, sold at a fixed 0.001 USDT in the [presale event](presale-event.md), raising a target of 100,000 USDT.
* **The remainder is split evenly** — 5% for community [airdrop events](../airdrop/overview.md) and 5% for the team.
