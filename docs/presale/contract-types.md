# Presale Contract Types

Every presale on DEXRise runs on one of two contract types, and the difference is simple but important: **when do you, the investor, receive your tokens?** Both types settle purchases on-chain and both deliver the raised funding to the project team only after the presale period has fully ended.

## Immediate Delivery Contracts

In an **immediate delivery** presale, the investor receives the tokens **at the moment of purchase**.

* You buy during the event → the tokens land in your possession right away.
* You can hold, stake, or otherwise manage your tokens from the instant the purchase is confirmed on-chain.

## Pending Delivery Contracts

In a **pending delivery** presale, the investor receives the tokens **after the presale period has fully ended**.

* You buy during the event → the purchase is recorded on-chain immediately, but token delivery waits until the event concludes.
* At the end of the presale, all purchased tokens are delivered to the investors.

## Side-by-side comparison

| | **Immediate Delivery** | **Pending Delivery** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **When you receive tokens** | Instantly, at the moment of purchase | After the presale period fully ends |
| **Purchase record on-chain** | Immediately at purchase | Immediately at purchase |
| **Funding to the project team** | After the presale fully ends | After the presale fully ends |
| **Post-purchase flexibility** | Tokens are usable right away | Tokens unlock at event end |

## Before you buy: check the contract type

Because the two models differ in delivery timing, always confirm **which contract type** a presale uses before participating:

* Buying into an **immediate delivery** presale means you own the tokens immediately.
* Buying into a **pending delivery** presale means your tokens are guaranteed by the purchase, but delivered only when the event ends.

{% hint style="info" %}
DEXRise's own token sale — the [DXR Presale Event](../dxr-token/presale-event.md) — runs on an **immediate delivery** contract: participants receive their DXR the moment they buy.
{% endhint %}
