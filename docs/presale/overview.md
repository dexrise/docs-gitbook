# Presale on DEXRise

The Presale service helps emerging projects **raise funding or initial liquidity** by running a token sale event on DEXRise. Projects list their presale, investors buy in during the event window, and every purchase settles **directly on-chain** — with full transparency and security.

## Key characteristics

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Raise funding or initial liquidity for emerging projects |
| **Buying** | Through an easy, simple **Buy Panel** — designed for complete beginners and professionals alike |
| **Execution** | All purchases settle **directly on-chain** |
| **Contract types** | Two models: [immediate delivery](contract-types.md#immediate-delivery-contracts) or [pending delivery](contract-types.md#pending-delivery-contracts) |
| **End conditions** | The presale ends when the **period expires** or the **allocated quantity sells out** — whichever comes first |

## The Buy Panel

The Buy Panel is deliberately simple. A participant only needs to specify the amount they want to buy — the panel handles the rest. There is nothing technical to configure, which makes joining a presale equally accessible to a first-time buyer and an experienced investor.

## What happens when the presale ends

A presale event runs until one of two things happens — whichever occurs first:

* **The event period ends**, or
* **The allocated quantity is fully sold.**

When the event ends, settlement follows fixed rules:

| Outcome | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Raised funding | Delivered to the project team **after the presale period has fully ended** |
| Unsold tokens | Delivered to the project team |
| Purchased tokens | Delivered per the event's [contract type](contract-types.md) — immediately at purchase, or after the presale fully ends |
