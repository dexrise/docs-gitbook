# FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about DEXRise. Every answer links to the page with the full details.

## Platform basics

### What is DEXRise?

DEXRise is a decentralized exchange platform that lets you trade on liquidity pools from major DEXs — PancakeSwap, Uniswap, SushiSwap and others — plus its own native pools, through one simple interface. On top of trading, it offers [Staking](staking/overview.md), [Presale](presale/overview.md) and [Airdrop](airdrop/overview.md) services, all executed directly on-chain. See [What is DEXRise?](introduction/what-is-dexrise.md).

### What makes DEXRise different from other DEXs?

Its exclusive **Floor Price Protection** mechanism — an unbreakable, automatically rising price floor that no other platform offers — along with its zero-liquidity listing model for projects and its fully on-chain services. See [Floor Price Protection](trading/floor-price-protection.md).

## Trading

### What is Floor Price Protection (FPP)?

A DEXRise-exclusive mechanism that gives a pair an unbreakable price floor, defined as a fixed percentage below the pair's All-Time High. Every new ATH raises the floor immediately and permanently, so the price always forms higher lows. Example: with a 50% floor, an ATH of $1.00 puts the floor at $0.50 — and the price can never go below it. Full explanation: [Floor Price Protection](trading/floor-price-protection.md).

### What happens when the price reaches the floor?

Selling operations **stop** at the floor. Reaching the floor acts as a strong buy signal — it marks the start of an accumulation phase and a new upward cycle. See [Floor Price Protection](trading/floor-price-protection.md#what-happens-when-the-price-reaches-the-floor).

### What are the trading fees?

The fee is applied **on the paid currency only**, and the rate depends on the pair type: **0.3%** on DEXRise pairs and **0.4%** on external pairs. See [Trading Fees](trading/trading-fees.md).

### What is Slippage Protection?

You set the maximum price change you are willing to tolerate on a trade. If the price change exceeds your limit, the transaction **fails automatically** — it never executes at a worse price than you accepted. See [Slippage Protection](trading/slippage-protection.md).

### What are Pair Ranks?

DEXRise classifies pairs and currencies into four tiers — Bronze (100+ votes), Silver (1,000+ votes), Gold (10,000+ votes + $1M daily volume) and Platinum (10,000+ votes + $10M daily volume). See [Pair Ranks](trading/ranks.md).

## Staking

### What is the difference between Flexible and Locked staking?

In **Flexible**, you can withdraw your deposit plus rewards at any time, with no restrictions. In **Locked**, the deposit stays until the full staking term ends — but rewards are claimable at any time, or can be re-deposited. See [Staking Types](staking/staking-types.md).

### How are staking rewards calculated?

DEXRise uses **APR** annualized over a full 365-day year, and rewards are **simple interest** — calculated on your deposited amount only, not compounded. Example: 1,000 tokens at 100% APR for 30 days ≈ 82.19 tokens. See [APR Mechanics](staking/apr-mechanics.md).

### Which tokens can be staked?

Staking pools support **standard tokens only** — Fee-on-Transfer tokens and similar mechanics are not supported. Projects listing a pool provide the tokens dedicated to it. See [Staking Pool Listing](listing/staking-listing.md).

## Presale

### When do I receive the tokens I bought in a presale?

It depends on the event's contract type: **immediate delivery** contracts deliver tokens at the moment of purchase, while **pending delivery** contracts deliver them after the presale period fully ends. See [Presale Contract Types](presale/contract-types.md).

### What happens if a presale doesn't sell out?

The event simply ends when its period expires — and any **unsold tokens are delivered to the project team**. The raised funding also reaches the team only after the presale has fully ended. See [Presale Lifecycle](presale/lifecycle.md).

## Airdrop

### How do I claim an airdrop?

Open the event's Claim Panel during the event period and claim with **one click** if you are eligible — the claim settles directly on-chain. Note that a single event can contain multiple distribution rounds. See [Airdrop on DEXRise](airdrop/overview.md).

Yes — the staking program is fully functional during the presale. The contract is **Flexible** (withdraw anytime, no restrictions), rewards are claimable anytime, and the first round runs at **100% APR** from November 3, 2026 to November 3, 2027. See [DXR Staking Event](dxr-token/staking-event.md).

**By February 1, 2027 at the latest — or earlier** if the presale sells out ahead of schedule. Follow the [official channels](contact.md) for launch announcements. See [DXR Trading Launch](dxr-token/trading-launch.md).

### What is DXR's launch price?

**0.001 USDT** — which is also the floor price itself. DXR starts trading at its unbreakable floor, so the first upward cycle begins immediately at launch, with initial liquidity of 500,000,000 DXR + 500,000 USDT and an initial market cap of $1,000,000. See [DXR Trading Launch](dxr-token/trading-launch.md).

## For projects

### How do I list my project on DEXRise?

Submit the [listing application form](https://forms.gle/V89E1Nd4eq4N2wtZA) for the service you want — trading pair, staking pool, presale or airdrop — and meet the requirements for that service. See the full section: [Listing Fees & Requirements](listing/overview.md).

### How much does listing cost?

**Trading pair:** $100 traditional, or $3,000 for the zero-liquidity model with Floor Price Protection. **Staking pool:** $50. **Presale:** $50 + 1% of tokens sold. **Airdrop:** $20 — free on BSC. See the [fee schedule](listing/overview.md#listing-fee-schedule).
