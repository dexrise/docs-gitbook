# Presale Listing

Listing a presale on DEXRise gives your project an on-chain token sale with a ready-made buying experience — an easy [Buy Panel](../presale/overview.md#the-buy-panel) that works for complete beginners and professional investors alike, with every purchase settling directly on-chain.

## Listing fees

| Fee type | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Listing fee** | **$50** |
| **Performance fee** | **1% of the tokens sold** |

The presale fee model has two parts: a flat **$50 listing fee**, plus a **1% fee on the tokens actually sold** during the event — so the performance fee scales with your sale's success.

## Listing requirements

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Application form** | Submit the [listing application form](https://forms.gle/V89E1Nd4eq4N2wtZA) |
| **Token provision** | Provide the **quantity allocated for sale** — the tokens that will be sold during the event |
| **Token standard** | **Standard tokens only** — Fee-on-Transfer tokens and similar mechanics are **not supported** |

## What your presale runs on

Your event runs on DEXRise's presale infrastructure:

* One of the two [contract types](../presale/contract-types.md) — **immediate delivery** (buyers receive tokens at purchase) or **pending delivery** (buyers receive tokens after the event ends).
* The event ends when the **period expires or the quantity sells out** — whichever comes first.
* After the event fully ends, the **raised funding and any unsold tokens are delivered to your team** — the standard [presale settlement rules](../presale/lifecycle.md).
* Every purchase executes **directly on-chain**.
