# Staking Pool Listing

Listing a staking pool on DEXRise lets your community lock their holdings into your project's pool and earn rewards. DEXRise staking supports pools built from **any two currencies** — different currencies or the same currency on both sides — so the pool design is up to you.

## Listing fee

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Staking pool listing fee** | **$50** |

## Listing requirements

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Application form** | Submit the [listing application form](https://forms.gle/V89E1Nd4eq4N2wtZA) |
| **Token provision** | Provide the **tokens dedicated to the staking pool** — this supply funds the rewards your pool pays out |
| **Token standard** | **Standard tokens only** — Fee-on-Transfer tokens and similar mechanics are **not supported** |

## What your pool runs on

A listed pool runs on DEXRise's staking infrastructure:

* One of the two [contract types](../staking/staking-types.md) — **Flexible** (deposit withdrawable anytime) or **Locked** (deposit committed until the term ends; rewards claimable anytime).
* Reward calculation follows the [APR mechanics](../staking/apr-mechanics.md) — annualized over 365 days, simple interest on the deposited amount.
* Users interact through the one-click [Staking Panel](../staking/overview.md#the-staking-panel) — deposit, withdraw and claim.
* All stake, withdraw and claim operations execute **directly on-chain**.

{% hint style="info" %}
Because pools can be built from **any two currencies**, you can design a pool where users stake your token and earn your token — or pair two different currencies in a single pool.
{% endhint %}
